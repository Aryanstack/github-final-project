# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge
We pledge to make participation in our project a welcoming and respectful experience for everyone.

## Our Standards
Examples of positive behavior include being respectful, accepting constructive criticism, and focusing on what is best for the community.

## Enforcement Responsibilities
Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards of acceptable behavior.

## Scope
This Code of Conduct applies within project spaces and when an individual officially represents the project.

## Enforcement
Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project maintainers.

## Enforcement Guidelines
Project maintainers will review reports and determine appropriate and fair corrective action.
